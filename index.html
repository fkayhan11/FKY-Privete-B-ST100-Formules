Manifesto · Chapter I

We make space audible. We make quietness tangible.

Aether & Tact is a multidisciplinary design practice operating at the intersection of sculpture, physical kinetics, and custom software.

We believe the modern screen is flat, overcrowded, and hyper-stimulated. Our practice rejects temporary attention-grabbing loops. We build bespoke digital installations and tactile identities that behave like physical architecture—reposing in silence, responding only to deliberate spatial presence.

Based out of a converted shipyard workshop in Copenhagen, we design objects and platforms that stand the test of physical gravity and digital time.
